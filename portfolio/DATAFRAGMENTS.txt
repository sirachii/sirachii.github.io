<p><strong>DATA FRAGMENTS</strong> is a digital media art series inspired by analog horror and lost media content.
I use personal photographs of myself and my family and intentionally glitch them to resemble recovered or damaged archival material.</p>

<p>The work explores memory, identity, and how familiar images become unstable when treated as corrupted records.</p>
