RING COLLECT!

PLAY GAME

It's simple! Just collect the rings to win! This game shows how pressure builds over time through a simple task that gets harder as you try to keep up.

Inspired by Sonic.EXE creepypasta aesthetics and simple arcade survival gameplay.
