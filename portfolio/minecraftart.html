minecraft art

For this project, I created a slice of cake in Minecraft.

This sculpture represents my childhood memories of birthdays. Cake always felt like the most important part, and I would get very specific about the flavor I wanted—sometimes even hoping for ice cream cake.

We would often end up with large cakes that were difficult to finish, since my family wasn’t big on sweets. Now, I prefer having a single slice for my birthday. It feels more meaningful and avoids waste while still keeping the tradition alive.
